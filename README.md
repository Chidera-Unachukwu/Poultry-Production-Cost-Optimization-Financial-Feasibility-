Poultry Production Cost Optimization & Financial Feasibility

Project Overview
This project applies data analytics to a real poultry production cycle in Anambra State, Nigeria, to evaluate operational performance, investigate cost-saving opportunities, and assess potential business expansion.

Objectives
Analyse historical production costs, revenue, and bird survival.

Compare ready-made feed costs with an illustrative in-house feed alternative.

Model production scenarios for 1,000, 3,000, and 5,000 birds.
Assess financial sensitivity to changes in selling prices, costs, and survival rates.

Develop actionable recommendations for cost management and business growth.

Tools
R: Statistical analysis and scenario modelling.
Excel: Data organization and calculations.
Power BI: KPI reporting and dashboard visualization(potential).

Key Findings
Historical survival rate: 96%.
Recorded revenue: ₦830,000.
Documented costs: ₦305,900.
Preliminary surplus: ₦524,100, before unrecorded expenses.
Illustrative feed-cost comparison suggests a potential reduction of approximately 51.7%.
Sensitivity analysis demonstrates how adverse market and production conditions can affect financial outcomes.

Business Value
The project demonstrates how data analytics can identify potential cost efficiencies, evaluate expansion scenarios, assess business risks, and translate quantitative findings into practical recommendations.

Limitations
Scaling and sensitivity results are illustrative model outputs, not actual business results. The feed-cost comparison requires validation through production trials, and complete profitability assessment requires additional operating and investment costs.

Skills Demonstrated
Data analysis, financial modelling, cost optimization, KPI development, scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, data visualization, and business problem-solving.
